The Keyguard Story

In December 2025, a chance encounter on the LRT between a mechanical engineering student and an assistive technology specialist sparked an idea: turn a simple volunteer effort into a thriving student group dedicated to providing affordable, custom assistive technology for campus and community members. From the beginning, this movement has been driven by two things: a real need, and a willingness to serve.

That initial need came from a student who required a $500 custom keyguard to type accurately for their coursework. Seeing an opportunity to help, Brian Sanchez—founder of the Assistive Technology Engineering Club (ATEC)—took on the design challenge to create a simple, cost-effective solution.

After extensive research, consultation, and prototyping, Brian designed a 3D-printed keyguard custom-fit to the student's exact keyboard, delivering the finished technology entirely free of charge in June 2026.

Reflecting on the impact of that first project, Brian Sanchez and Kristine Johnson realized they could empower other design-oriented students to do the same: provide direct, accessible solutions for individuals needing academic or personal accommodations.

Today, ATEC operates on a dual commitment to hands-on service and engineering mentorship. By pairing practical design education with direct community delivery, the club continues to break down the cost and customization barriers that keep so many people from accessing the technology they need.

Mission, Vision and Values

Mission

  • Provide personalized, low-to-no cost 3D printed assistive technology
  • Serve personal and/or academic accessibility needs of groups or individuals
  • Cultivate the next generation of technically skilled and compassionate engineers

Vision

  • Establish a permanent student-led hub in engineering for assistive device delivery
  • Collaborate with community partners to broaden access to assistive devices
  • Engage engineering students year-round for impactful assistive technology development

Values

  • User-centered Design
  • Interdisciplinary Effort
  • Safety and Integrity
  • Accessibility and Equity
  • Advocacy and Awareness
