Makers Making Change

Some assistive technologies may already exist!

Makers Making Change is a program by the Neil Squire Society who have a catalogue of DIY assistive technology. You can either make the assistive technology yourself by following MMC's build instructions or submit a device request where a volunteer will build and deliver the device for you.

University of Alberta Accessibility and Accommodations

For students at the University of Alberta, the Accessibility and Accommodations team provides resources to help remove barriers for students. Take a look at their website to learn more about what they may provide!
